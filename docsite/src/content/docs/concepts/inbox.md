Whenever you want a short, easily accessed, quick way to view chat sessions across your entire world, regardless of
the island or agent, you can use the **Inbox** view. You can get to this view via contextual buttons throughout the interface,
and you can also get there by clicking on the Citadel and choosing _Inbox_.

When you want to see and interact with the full chat transcript of an agent's session, the Inbox is the tool to use.

## Tool Calls

Each tool call the agent makes shows as a chip where it happened in the conversation. Click a chip to see what the call did: its input, what came back, and how long it took.

In a long conversation, the chips can bury what the agent said. Press _Collapse tool calls_ above the conversation, and each run of calls between the agent's messages becomes one badge that counts them, such as _5 tool calls_. Click a badge to show that run's chips, and click it again to hide them. _Expand tool calls_ shows every chip again.

Collapsing applies to one conversation, and lasts until you reload the page. What you were reading stays in view when you collapse or expand.
