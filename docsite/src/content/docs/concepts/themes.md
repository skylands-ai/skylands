Themes can be easily added to Skylands by submitting new materials and styles in the Skylands core repository.
Right now, Skylands comes with two built-in visual themes:

* **Sky** (default) - The citadel is a castle surrounded by floating islands in a bright blue sky.
* **Sci-Fi** - The citadel is a space port floating in space, surrounded by space stations. Ships are not airships, but rather space-themed ships.
