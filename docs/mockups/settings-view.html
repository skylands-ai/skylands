Implementation plan

Moves the citadel panel’s Islands, Approvers, app install, Notifications, and NATS sections into a Settings view of their own, beside World and Inbox.

What goes where

  • Citadel panel keeps its header, its facts (world, owner key, islands connected, waiting on you, version), and World theme, which changes the view right beside it. A last line points to Settings.
  • Settings › World: Islands (the table, with a Last seen column now that there’s room, and Add a host), Approvers (the list, Add an approver, Revoke, this browser’s storage, and the ones that no longer approve), and NATS.
  • Settings › This browser: Notifications, and App, which takes the install offer out of the Approvers section.

Changes

  1. store.ts: view becomes 'world' | 'inbox' | 'settings', kept in skylands.view as now. A new settingsPage (islands, approvers, nats, notifications, app) is kept in skylands.settingsPage, and starts at Islands.
  2. index.html: #modeSettings as the third button of the view switch, and <section class="settings" id="settings" hidden> in .main.
  3. main.ts: renderMode hides the stage, the panel, the crumbs, and the inbox for Settings, and pauses the world as the inbox does. The veil covers Settings as it covers the other views. Settings redraws from a deps list, as renderPanel does, and keeps its scroll positions.
  4. New web/src/settings.ts: the page list and the five pages. It takes islandTable, addHost, leafAddress, natsFacts, and notifySwitch out of panels/world.ts. approversSection splits so the install offer goes to App. The list’s counts and states come from the same state the pages use.
  5. panels/world.ts: renderWorldPanel drops the four sections and the arguments only they used. The world entry of panelDeps drops notify, push, pushError, install, and the approver and invite fields, and takes the island counts in place of the island list, so the citadel panel redraws less. It keeps server, which the Healthy pill reads.
  6. A row of the islands table opens its island: nav.select(host), then setView('world').
  7. Pages don’t fold, since each holds one topic. foldSection stays for other panels; the islands, approvers, and nats ids in skylands.folded go unused.
  8. App shows what this browser can do whatever the install state is: the offer, “installed”, “this is the app”, or that the browser hasn’t offered it. As today, only an approver gets the offer.
  9. CSS: the .settings* rules at the top of this file’s <style>, and .modes .btn + .btn, so a three-button switch keeps single borders. The group headings use the world glyph from parts.ts, and a new browser glyph (a window with its tab bar) joins it there.
  10. Phone: unchanged. It has no view switch, and keeps its own Notifications switch under the list.
  11. Docs: “the citadel’s panel” becomes Settings in docsite operations/islands.md, service.md, and notifications.md, in docs/NATS.md, and in DESIGN.md (the World row of the levels table, Add a host, and the NATS server facts).
