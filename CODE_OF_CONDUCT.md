# Code of Conduct

This applies to everyone in the Skylands project's spaces, maintainers included: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Guidelines

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or experience. Harassment and abuse aren't tolerated.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Share only content you have the right to share, and respect other people's privacy. That includes what's in the logs, transcripts, and diffs you post.
- Stay on topic, and don't spam.
- Ask for a review by mentioning someone on GitHub, not by email or direct message.
- Follow the law.

## Enforcement

Maintainers enforce these guidelines in three steps:

1. The first violation gets a public reminder of the guidelines.
2. The second gets a warning that another violation means removal.
3. The third can mean a block from the project's repositories, depending on what happened.

The same rules apply to everyone. A violation is forgiven after six months without another, and a minor slip gets guidance rather than a step. Spam accounts are blocked at the first occurrence. Threats, harassment, publishing someone's private information, and illegal content are acted on at once, with no steps and no forgiveness period.

## Reporting

For something in the open, mention a maintainer on the issue or pull request.

For anything you don't want to raise in public, report it privately from the repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/skylands-ai/skylands/security/advisories/new), and say in the title that it's a conduct report. Only the maintainers can see it. Appeal a decision the same way. Maintainers don't share who made a report.

Content that breaks GitHub's own terms can also be [reported to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
